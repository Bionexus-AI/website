Pollen

A multi-resolution molecular simulation set for docking, complex formation likelihood, epitope identification, competitive binding, concentration-aware analysis, and mechanistic signal exploration.

Simulation that moves beyond a single docking pose.

Pollen uses ARBD-centered docking and simulation workflows to analyze molecular association across binding targets, binders, ions, and other third-party molecules. It is built for teams that need mechanistic insight, not only a ranked docking output.

The simulation set supports atomic-resolution epitope identification, concentration-dependent observables, multi-binder competition, post-association conformational changes, mechanical signal transduction, electron/proton transfer exploration, and hybrid quantum-classical workflows.

Association

Surface-wide binding likelihood

Estimate complex formation likelihood across simulated molecular surfaces at atomic resolution.

Competition

Multiple binder species

Simultaneously model competing binders and generate statistics on relative efficacy.

Mechanism

Signal and transfer pathways

Explore conformational shifts, mechanical signal transduction, and proton or electron transfer paths.
