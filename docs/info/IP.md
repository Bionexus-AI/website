HLA inception:

- Offers a physics-informed deep learning model for immunogenicity analyses.
- Provides additional evaluations of immunogenicity to existing models of Zara’s team.
  Bio-transformer:
- Provides biophysics-based embeddings for amino acid sequences.
- Enables the use of biophysical properties to condition existing sequence-based only deep learning model of
  Zara’s team.
  Pollen – multi-resolution molecular simulation set:
- Provides complex formation likelihoods among binding targets, binders, and any other third-party molecules,
  such as ions, across the whole surface of each simulated molecule at atomic resolutions for identification of
  epitopes.
- Enables concentration-based simulations and analyses to offer insights on the dosage-dependence for any
  observable that is derivable from molecular features.
- Allow simultaneous calculations of multiple species of binders, providing statistics on a binder’s efficacy in a
  competition-enabled environment.
- Samples post-association conformational changes and associated mechanical signal transduction pathways at
  identified epitopes.
- Explores transfer pathways for electrons and protons.
- Allows hybrid quantum-classical calculations (e.g. QM/MM + MD).
