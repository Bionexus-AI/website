Platform

AI that starts from molecular physics, not just sequence scale.

Many discovery workflows ask AI to infer molecular behavior from sparse, noisy, or indirect data. Bionexus.AI is developing representations that encode electrostatic landscapes, amino acid biophysical properties, molecular association behavior, and simulation-derived context so models can learn from richer physical signal.

Deep capabilities

From binding likelihood to signal transfer, the stack is built for mechanism.

Pollen is designed to move beyond a single docking pose. It can evaluate complex formation likelihood across molecular surfaces, explore competition among multiple binders, model concentration-dependent observables, and sample post-association conformational changes.

Surface-wide association Binding target, binder, ions, and third-party molecule interactions at atomic resolution.
Dosage-aware analysis Concentration-based simulations for observables derived from molecular features.
Competitive binding Simultaneous multi-species calculations that estimate binder efficacy in crowded environments.
Mechanistic pathways Conformational change, mechanical signal transduction, electron and proton transfer exploration.
Hybrid physics Placeholder for QM/MM, molecular dynamics, and hybrid quantum-classical workflows.
Implementation muscle Cloud, HPC, simulation, ML, and workflow engineering for client-specific ecosystems.
