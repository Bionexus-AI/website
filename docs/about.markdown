Bionexus.AI is a stealth biotech startup building physics-informed AI systems for immune and molecular discovery.

Our work connects electrostatic protein representations, biophysical amino acid embeddings, molecular docking, multi-resolution simulation, and modern machine learning. The goal is to help discovery teams reason about molecular systems where structure, charge, dynamics, concentration, competition, and immune context matter.

Current public-facing pillars:

- HLA-Inception: physics-informed deep learning for immunogenicity analysis.
- Bio-transformer: biophysics-based embeddings for amino acid sequences and sequence-first models.
- Pollen: multi-resolution molecular simulation for association likelihood, epitope identification, competitive binding, concentration-aware analysis, conformational change, and hybrid physics workflows.
- Consulting and implementation: cloud, HPC, machine learning, and computational biophysics workflow engineering.
